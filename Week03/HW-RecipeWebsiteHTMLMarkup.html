The Best Breakfast Sandwich

Adapted from the famed cafe in Los Angeles, Sqirl.

Total Time 30 minutes Yield 1 serving

This light and geometrical sandwich is perfect for breakfast.

Ingredients

  • Fluffy Milk Bun
    • Bread Flour
    • Honey
    • Instant Yeast
    • Salt
    • Egg
    • Warm Milk
    • Unsalted Butter
  • Organic Sheeted Egg
    • Egg
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Butter
  • New School Cheddar
  • Hash Brown Square
    • Shaved Potato
    • Olive Oil
    • Salt
  • Fermented Jalapeno Aioli
    • Mayonnaise
    • Laco-Fermented Jalapenos
    • Fresh Garlic
    • Fresh Cilantro
    • Lime Juice

Directions

  1. Make Milk Bun
    1. Add ingredients to stand mixer.
    2. Mix and knead.
    3. Form dough into ball and preheat oven to 300 F.
    4. Let dough rise for several hours.
    5. Bake at 300 F for 22 minutes, or until fully cooked.
  2. Make Sheeted Egg
    1. Crack eggs with butter, salt, and pepper and whisk until combined.
    2. Pour into a butter-greased pan on medium heat.
    3. Gently fold egg into a square.
    4. Season to taste with additional salt & pepper.
  3. Make Lacto-Fermented Jalapeno Aioli
    1. Combine fermented jalapenos, garlic, cilantro, lime juice, and mayonnaise into a small food processor.
    2. Pulse until smooth and creamy, making sure the peppers and garlic are fully incorporated.
    3. Taste and adjust the salt or lime juice as needed.
  4. Cook the Hash Brown
    1. Shred the potato.
    2. Squeeze out extra moisture.
    3. Add cooking oil and butter into a pan on medium heat.
    4. Add the potato, salt, and pepper into pan and fold into a square.
  5. Cut the avocado into thin slices.
  6. Assemble the sandwich
    1. Cut the bun in half.
    2. On the bottom bun, spread the aioli.
    3. Place the hash brown, egg, and avocado on top.
    4. Spread more aioli on the top bun and close the sandwich.
  7. Enjoy!
